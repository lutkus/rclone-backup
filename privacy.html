Privacy Policy

This application is a personal-use OAuth application used with rclone to back up a KeePass database from a Mac to Google Drive.

Information accessed

The application accesses files in the user's Google Drive as authorized by the user. It does not collect, sell, or share personal information with third parties.

Use of information

Google Drive access is used solely to perform the user's personal backup operation.

Data retention

The application does not maintain a separate database of user information. Files remain in the user's Google Drive according to the user's own Google Drive settings and actions.

Contact

This application is maintained for personal use.
